Introduction

Come and have a fun time with Protect Earth! We appreciate volunteers who are able to come for part of the day, a whole day or even the whole weekend. No previous experience is needed and children are welcome.

Protect Earth is a registered charity, helping farmers and landowners increase tree cover and biodiversity on their land. We plant trees on unused and degraded farmland, use trees to provide shade and shelter for crops and livestock, create wildflower meadows, and help with tree maintenance for saplings that are struggling.

What is happening?

We bought this land several years ago thanks to some very generous donations. We are creating a woodland on part of the site to benefit wildlife including declining bird species such as the pied fly catcher and the wood warbler. Since planting we have had droughts and excessive temperatures. Whilst some saplings have done well, we have lost quite a few others, so we will be replacing 1000 trees this weekend. Root dip and mulch will helo them to establish and prevent the soil from drying out around the young trees. Planting earlier in the winter will help too.

We love it when the local community gets involved in their area and we hope the same will happen here, giving us a chance to catch up with friends we've made, and learn what people love about their communities, while talking to them about enriching local biodiversity.

What do I need to bring/ know?

No previous experience is necessary; we’ll show you what to do and provide all the necessary equipment. Please come dressed for the weather.

Please bring your own lunch if you are planning to stay all day, snacks, and drinks including water.

Access to the site is up a steep track which can get muddy in winter. Please wear boots or wellies with a good grip.

Full details will be sent out a week before the event, including map links and meeting place.

Will there be parking?

There is some parking near the site and in several laybys along the main road.

Whenever possible we encourage volunteers to car share, use public transportation, or even cycle, to keep emissions down and avoid stressing parking space.

What happens if we need to cancel?

Bad weather is difficult to plan for. In general, we will continue to plant if it’s raining, but if a severe storm is forecast or the ground is frozen, we will have to postpone an event. We will try and notify you as soon as we possibly can, and you’ll be invited to the rescheduled event.

If you need to cancel, that's fine, we understand that plans change. But please let us know so that we can plan the day.
