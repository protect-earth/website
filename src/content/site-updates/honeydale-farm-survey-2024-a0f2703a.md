The three experimental shelterbelts have had quite different success rates.  Deer guards were used to protect the saplings in the first shelterbelt and these trees have the best survival rate with 71%. The second shelterbelt was protected by deer fencing and some hedging but had a disappointing survival rate of 57%.  The third shelterbelt was protected by hedgerow but performed the worst of the three with a 49% survival rate, bringing the overall survival rate for this site to only 57%.

This is well below our guaranteed survival rate of 80% so restocking will be required here as soon as possible.
