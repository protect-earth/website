Previous conifer clearing sessions had removed many of the smaller conifers, so this year we just had help from our trusty work party members, most of whom join us on the third weekend of every month to help with a variety of necessary tasks needed to restore this wonderful woodland. Both our October and November Work Party sessions were spent in conifer removal when we were delighted to discover more young saplings hiding under the conifers. There is now much more light in the understorey giving not only saplings, but other native woodland plants a chance to grow and spread.

After the November session local people were invited to come and take away a free tree to use as a Christmas tree with a character all of it’s own, and we also donated trees to local charities and colleges who were very pleased to have them.
