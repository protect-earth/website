After helping the Screech Owl Sanctuary and Animal Park plant a new woodland, shelterbelt and hedgerow last February we were very pleased to hear that their tree planting project had won the Trees for Nature & Growth (Farmers & Landowners) category at the Cornwall & Isles of Scilly Awards in December. This was a delightful surprise for us all.

The owners contacted us to thank us again. “We would like to recognise your part in enabling us to get this award and thank you for your support in sourcing the trees and all the hard work in the planting of the trees. We have acknowledged you on our Facebook and X post.”
