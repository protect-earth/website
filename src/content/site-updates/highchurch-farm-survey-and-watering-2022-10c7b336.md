We don’t normally carry out a survival survey in the first year, but we were very concerned about these trees in the face of this hot dry summer. When we arrived, we found that many saplings were dry and brown. Even those that had clearly been growing were showing at least some brown leaves. At least 50% were dead so we decided to act.

A week later we returned with tanks of water to try to save those saplings with the best chance of survival.

We will return as early as possible in the planting season to restock, thus giving the new saplings the best opportunity to settle in. Hopefully, 2023 will not be another drought year.
