In February, 2025 we led a group of volunteers from the Bourton-on-the-Water  branch of Zenergi to plant 125 new trees to replace those lost in the previous year. They also carried out some general maintenance, removing grass from inside guards, and replacing or repairing damaged guards and stakes as required. There were quite a few brambles to contend with amongst the grass so care was required.

We found some very pregnant sheep in the nearby fields so the volunteers were a little quieter than usual, and were careful not to spook them into an early labour.  Tthe group worked really hard and even managed to finish earlier than expected,

The volunteers said “We loved the day planting with you! Thank you for the opportunity. The landowner was very friendly and hospitable, opening his home to us and providing us with pots of tea & coffee and snacks.”

The landowner was equally enthusiastic. “It all went marvellously, as far as I am concerned. And I am glad the volunteers had a good time. They sent me some photos and I have done a post about it in my social media. It mentions Protect Earth and all they have done, for which we are very grateful!”
