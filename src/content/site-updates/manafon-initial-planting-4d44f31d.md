The objective here was to improve the site for biodiversity by planting a mix of native tree and shrub species including Oak, Wild Cherry, Rowan and Birch. The new planting incorporated gaps, glades and include shrub species to give the woodland a diverse and natural structure.

This site is in the middle of the Welsh countryside and is accessed by a narrow single track road with little parking in the planting field, so the landowner offered to pick up volunteers from a car park in the nearby village.

 There was drama when the nursery rang to say they had had problems, firstly with frozen ground and then with staff shortages, and wouldn’t be able lift the trees in time. However, when we explained that we had eager volunteers waiting to plant them the nursery kindly pushed our order to the top of the list. The trees were due to be delivered on Tuesday, and finally arrived at around 3pm on Friday afternoon, just in time!

The weather was extremely wet in the week before our weekend planting, but stopped in time for the landowner to cut the grass the day before, which helped enormously with planting the trees. Planting day was rather overcast but remained dry, but breezy.

We struggled to find volunteers for this out of the way spot but those who did had a good time. One volunteer responded as follows: “Really friendly atmosphere, lovely people”.

We had hoped to plant all the trees in one day, but with only 5 volunteers it wasn’t possible. Undeterred, the team stayed behind and spent Sunday morning finishing the job.
