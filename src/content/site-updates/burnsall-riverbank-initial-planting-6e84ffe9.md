We’re planting here on the banks of the River Wharfe near Burnsall, North Yorkshire. There are many benefits gained by establishing riparian trees, including: the reduction of soil erosion; the creation of a buffer zone against the leaching and surface run-off of pollution and nutrients; providing shade and maintaining oxygen levels during periods of low flow; as well as creating shelter and feeding opportunities for fish. However, the main aim of this project is to stabilise three distinct sections of  the ‘Higher Bank’ – an ‘effectively linear’ 200m section. ‘Middle Grassy Bank’ – an area of 840 m2. And, ‘Sand and Gravel Bank Area’ – an area of 2267 m2 at river level.

With this project being up north, our trustee, Andy, based in Scotland, was down for the planting. With this being a mid-spring planting, it was decided to use irrigation to give the saplings the best start.
