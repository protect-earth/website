The landowners were happy to carry out this survey for us. The first area Protect Earth planted had done especially well with few dead trees, and most of the saplings already growing out of the guards. The second area had more failures, with the part nearest the farmhouse performing better than that further away. No particular species seemed to have suffered more. There have also been a few additional trees (field maple) that have self seeded from the nearby green lane in the lower survival area.

Whist carrying out the survey, the landowners also carried out some simple maintenance, straightening guards and stakes as needed.

The landowners had placed a couple of corrugated metal panels in amongst the trees and have seen field voles and shrews nesting in the shelter belt areas, plus barred grass snakes. Lots of other wild plants have also established - most interestingly toothwort spotted last year! A local Beaver Scout group visited to forage for sticks & pine cones in a nearby more established woodland, then they made the big ‘bug hotel’ to add to the area. The landowners reported having seen plenty of butterflies and moths including small heath, meadow brown and  painted lady butterflies, and silver y moths and said that the whole area is buzzing with bees, crickets and other insects.
