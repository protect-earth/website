This site is doing quite well, thanks to the recent restocking and maintenance day, with an overall survival rate of 85%. Those that have died, appeared to have died recently, with dried leaves still on the branches. This is presumably due to the drought and excessive heat of the last few weeks.

The saplings that were planted nearest to the existing hedge are becoming swallowed up by it, although the saplings continue to do well at the moment, with saplings growing well above the guards.

It was good to observe butterflies including painted lady, small blue and meadow brown visiting the site during our visit.
