Common Bird Census (CBC) territory mapping of the breeding birds at Pantpurlais was carried out on 15/06/23. This is the third year we have had a survey carried out here.

Twenty-six different species were recorded to be holding territory on site, which is an increase of three from last year and two more that 2021.  Species included: blackbird, blue tit, chaffinch, coat tit, crow, goldcrest, goldfinch, greater spotted woodpecker, great tit, meadow pipit, mistle thrush, nuthatch, pied flycatcher, pied wagtail, raven, redstart, robin, siskin, skylark, song thrush, stock dove, stonechat, treecreeper, tree pipit, willow warbler, wood pigeon, wren

 The pied flycatchers, first seen in 2021 are back, which is good news as there was no sign of them during the survey in 2022. Song thrush was a new species but there were two territories of them this year. Tree pipit was another new species here taking the total to 25 species (plus a skylark spotted just off site, just off site). Stonechats with 3 fledged young was good to see.

The area around the site was not surveyed, but house sparrow, house martin, swift and spotted flycatchers are likely to be around the buildings.
