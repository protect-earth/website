On a beautiful, sunny day in July, 2024 our site surveyor made a visit to this property to see how our new trees were getting on. He was pleased to see that 91% were still alive and growing well.

The landowner has worked hard to maintain this site. He has endeavoured to visit after strong winds armed with a hammer to adjust and make good stakes, etc. He also paid to have the entire area mowed, which has given the trees a chance to grow without facing competition from grass which can also absorb the nutrients from the soil.

The success of this site shows how important regular maintenance can be. We need to do nothing further here for now.
