This farm near Box is run by a charity which host farm-based residential and day visit programmes for young people in partnership with their schools and youth organisations at 7 locations across England and Wales. The young people are able to experience the countryside, work on the farm and enjoy some peace and quiet.

The hedgerow here was rather sparce so needed some infill planting. Once that was done we extended the hedgerow by a further 100 metres. We planted a mix of native trees and shrubs, protecting them with spirals and guards, creating a natural barrier for the farm, and a corridor with food and shelter for wildlife to improve biodiversity.
