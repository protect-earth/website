Once the shelterbelt had been planted here at Springmere, we moved on to planting 3000 mixed trees and shrubs to create a total of 600m of hedgerow to aid in increasing biodiversity in this very exposed spot.

We bought larger bare root whips than we normally do, to give them the best chance of survival.
