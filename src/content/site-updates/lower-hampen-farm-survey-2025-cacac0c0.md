The survival rate of 73% was rather disappointing this year, but not entirely unexpected after the dry summer we have had. Gaps could be seen throughout the shelterbelt.

Some trees had done surprisingly well, whilst others were still very short with browning leaves, caused by the heat rather than autumnal changes.

To fill in the gaps, 320 new saplings were planted.
