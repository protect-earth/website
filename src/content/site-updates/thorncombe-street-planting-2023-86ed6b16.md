This was our first planting at Thornton Street. The woodland had  a lot of Cherry Laurel in it which the landowners had removed before we arrived. The primary issue with Cherry Laurel is that it’s just too good at growing! It outpaces almost all other native species planted alongside it, and steals all the nutrients, leading to a monoculture. Removing these shrubs had left many gaps in the woodland.

 We planned two days of planting, with corporate volunteers from Talking Tables on the first day, and from TML Partners on the second. With Steve marking out where the saplings should be planted using bamboo canes, we were able to plant some local variants of trees in the woodland gaps, ensuring a much wider variety in the immediate vicinity of the planting area. We were also able to create a narrow shelterbelt along the side of the road. By creating these areas of diversity, it means that all manner of wildlife are able to make their homes in and amongst the trees.

The volunteers all had a great time. “Super day - highly rewarding and our team came away feeling they had made a positive impact. Phil was superb throughout.”
