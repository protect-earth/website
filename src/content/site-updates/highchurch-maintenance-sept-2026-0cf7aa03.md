Seven corporate volunteers from local company Mulberry volunteered with us for a second time. They joined forces with three of our regular volunteers to help us carry out maintenance on another section of trees on this large site. They spread mulch, straightened and repaired stakes and guards, and reported any dead trees. The landowner and our team carried out a little strimming around the saplings which needed it most.

Facilities at Highchurch are excellent. The landowner had tea, coffee and biscuits ready and made sure we had everything we needed.

The Mulberry group were a really lovely, enthusiastic bunch, getting on with it from the outset. We took a lunch break at 1pm and they were eager to get back to it and keen to work through until 4pm. We didn’t quite finish all we intended to but everyone was very thorough and did an excellent job. The Mulberry volunteers took a group photo at the end, clearly enjoyed the day and all said they'd love to come back.
