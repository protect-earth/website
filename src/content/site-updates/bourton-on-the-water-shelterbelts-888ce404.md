We were lucky enough to have a beautiful sunny February day to plant the two shelterbelts and a small woodland required here, which was perfect for the videos taken using a drone. Instead of our normal four-row shelterbelts, we planted just three rows, as here in the Cotswolds the farm is quite sheltered We planted 15 each of Beech, Hornbeam, Wild Cherry and Silver Birch for the centre of each shelter belt, then filled in along the edges with smaller shrubs to create denser shelterbelts and a more natural look. Offsetting the shrubs helps to break up the wind flow once the saplings have matured. We used two cardboard guards and wooden guards on the trees for better protection, and bamboo canes and clear spirals on the shrubs.

The wonderful landowners kept our team and our volunteers going with hot drinks and an amazing lunch. Much revived, everyone returned to work with a will.

Once the shelterbelts were completed we planted around 100 trees and shrubs in a small triangle of land approximately 0.65 of an acre which will help to screen the farm from the road, and given time will rewild naturally.

Thanks to our hardworking volunteers we were able to complete all three areas in one day. Everyone had fun, and went home knowing that they had done something positive to improve the environment.
