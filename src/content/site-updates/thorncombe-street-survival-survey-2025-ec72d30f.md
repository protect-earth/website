Angus visited in August to carry out a survival survey. He found that across the site the three planted areas are doing well considering the dry weather. This was partly thanks to the landowners who have maintained all three sites very well. The least successful area was the woodland area with a survival rate of 70%  but the area by the barn had an 88% survival rate which was much better. The linear patch of wood on the hilltop is also doing well.

We will go back and have a woodland maintenance day and plant a few trees to keep the overall survival rate up in that area.
