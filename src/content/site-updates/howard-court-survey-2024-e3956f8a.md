While the team were at Howard Court to carry out some maintenance they carried out our annual survey too. Last winter the National Trust had donated some trees to raise the survival rate by 16%, which was really helpful. These, and our original trees, are now growing really well with an overall survival rate of 95%. The cooler weather this year has definitely helped, and the maintenance we carried out today will help too.
