While we were onsite creating the woodland here, our volunteers also helped us to plant a 300m hedgerow consisting of a mix of hawthorn, hazel, holly and guelder rose which will serve to link other areas of the site, providing safe passage, food and shelter for a variety of wildlife. It will eventually provide shelter for the sheep here too.

There was a badger sett on the edge of the field where we were planting the hedgerow, so we made sure not to plant over it, and to leave some room either side so that the roots of the hedge would not impinge on the sett.
