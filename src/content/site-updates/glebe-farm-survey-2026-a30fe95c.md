We were pleased to see that rest of the older trees from our original planting in 2023 are well established with branches well above the top of the guards on both parts of the site. The saplings on the outskirts of both areas have fared less well, with grass growing vigorously within the guards in some places, and thistles swamping young trees in others. Despite that, there is still some encouraging growth to be seen.

There was no evidence of significant deer browsing, even on the saplings where the guards have been removed. These trees continue to do well.
