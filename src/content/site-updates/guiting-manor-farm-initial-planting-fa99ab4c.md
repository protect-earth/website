The Guiting Manor Amenity Trust wanted a shelterbelt along the edge of the farm which looked out over the valley.

With volunteers from FWAG SW and help from the landowner’s own team we planted 2734 trees and shrubs to create a 582m shelterbelt. It is often worth taking the time to set out string as a marker when a straight shelterbelt is required, and plotting where each tree needs to be with bamboo canes ensures even planting.  The ground was wet underfoot, which is no bad thing when planting new saplings, but the rain held off for most of the day.

This shelterbelt will help increase crop productivity, protect the sheep, and also benefit wildlife by offering safe passage over what was bare ground, in addition to providing food and shelter throughout the seasons.
