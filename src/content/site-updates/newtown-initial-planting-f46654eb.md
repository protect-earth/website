We planted 825 trees and shrubs here over approximately one hectare, with random spacings of 2.5m to 3m between saplings. Some areas by the streem were very wet so we planted  Alders  and a few Downy Birch here, and left the rest as clearings. Elsewhere, we planted predominately Sessile Oak, with a Holly and Hazel understory, and also scattered in some Downy Birch and Rowan.  Hawthorn and Blackthorn were planted around the perimeter of the main planting for shelter. There were some overhead power cables across the site so we avoided planting under them, and only planted smaller shrubs nearby.

There were already small pockets of woodland nearby and the new planting will provide further nectar, berries and nuts - great sources of food for a wide range of species. Both Green and Great-spotted Woodpecker were seen during planting, along with Raven and Red Kite overhead.
