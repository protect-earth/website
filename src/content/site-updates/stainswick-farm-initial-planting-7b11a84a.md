This shelterbelt was planted in early 2022, a mix of 559 native trees and shrubs, to create a windbreak and natural border along the edge of this field.  Unfortunately no photographs were taken at the time. The photos here were taken several months later and already there is some evidence of growth, although we will need to keep a close eye on them due to the surrounding grass and thistles competing for light and nutrients.
