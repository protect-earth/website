Pennyhill Park Hotel was the third in the Exclusive Hotels chain we have planted for. We created two small woodlands in the hotel’s grounds with 1,650 trees and shrubs; the plants were a mix of native broadleaf species suitable for the conditions, along with some Scots Pine. Hotel staff members volunteered in three-hour shifts and volunteers from Total Active Hub also joined us for half a day to help with the planting. They all appreciated the excellent refreshments and lunch provided by the hotel.

Trees were planted with 3m spacing but avoiding rows, lines and any uniformity with small clearings or glades. There was a mix of 90 per cent tree species and 10 per cent shrub species to create a natural looking woodland with a canopy of taller trees and shrub understory. We were very lucky with the weather with beautiful sunshine on the first day. Clouds rolled in on the second day but it remained dry and we were finished by lunchtime.

Our contact at the hotel said “The information beforehand was really great, Steve and Andy on the day were very helpful and explained everything well. Thank you”

The volunteers from Total Active Hub had a good time. “It's an absolute pleasure, we had such a fantastic morning. We had such positive feedback from our volunteers regarding the day so we'll certainly be looking to volunteer and do more with you in the future.”
