On this wet Sunday morning only one volunteer turned up to help, but fortunately we had planted many of the trees the day before and we only had the woodland on our neighbours’ farm left to plant.

We dotted the saplings at random, roughly three metres apart, with shrubs in clusters and around perimeter of the new planting to provide protection using a ratio of 70% trees to 30% shrubs. We left some gaps, especially on the eastern edge of the new planting near existing trees. With a low stocking density of 1111 trees per hectare there will be plenty of opportunity for natural regeneration from the adjoining woodland area.

Our sole volunteer was friendly and worked well with the rest of the team, even saying that they would be happy to volunteer with us again.
