We planted 2000 trees here on another of our farms in the Wiltshire area near Box and Colerne to form a 500m hedgerow.  Hedgerows have been disappearing in the English countryside since the 1940s, and are only recently starting to make a comeback. They act as a barrier and shelter for livestock, as well as providing food, shelter and safe passage for many species of wildlife from mammals to invertebrates, and of course the help to sequester carbon. We also popped in twelve larger trees to begin a small coppice which will expand over time.

This was all funded by our donors, and planted with the help of the Cotswold Wardens volunteers.
