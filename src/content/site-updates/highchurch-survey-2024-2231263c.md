When we visited Highchurch Farm this year we were pleased to see that survival rates had stabilised, with only a 3% loss since last year. Most trees were doing well with quite a few well out of their guards.

Grass remains a problem so we will plan for a work part next summer to remove grass from guards, and to repair and replace stakes and guards where needed.
