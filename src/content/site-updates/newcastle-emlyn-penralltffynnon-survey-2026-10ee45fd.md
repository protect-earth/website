It was heartening to see trees such as the rowan and blackthorn full of berries and well out of their guards. Hazel was also doing quite well.

Unfortunately, the bracken remains a big problem on this property and although we could see some trees growing strongly through the undergrowth, most of the trees our surveyor could find under the bracken were dead. It was not possible to survey the entire site because the bracken was too dense and almost two metres in height in places.

The landowner has tried crushing the bracken and even scything it but the site is steeply sloping and difficult to manage. He does feel that he is making some progress with removing it.
