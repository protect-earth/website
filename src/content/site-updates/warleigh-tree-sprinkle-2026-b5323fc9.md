In March 2026 Phil took a group of volunteers to Warleigh and planted 500 new trees! Species planted included Black Alder and Aspen. With Warleigh being a mixture of land types, it’s important we plant the right trees in the right places. A big thank you to our funding sponsor Bates Wells, for helping to fund this event.

All trees were planted with biodegradable guards and stakes, with even the smallest volunteers helping! It was a real family day out, and everyone is looking forward to returning to the site for the next stretch of work.
