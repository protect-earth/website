The oaks, while still small, are looking healthy, and the hawthorn and downy birch are also doing well. Encouragingly, the poplars are thriving. Considering the weather conditions and the heavy clay soil, which is prone to cracking and breaking, the site is looking positive overall.

In terms of tree survival, the survival rate remained at 75% which is very encouraging.

In addition to the recent work party, we are planning to continue maintenance of this large site in September, and also restock a small number of trees. We will top up with more trees once we are further into in the planting season.
