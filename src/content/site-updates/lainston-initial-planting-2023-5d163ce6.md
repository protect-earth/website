In November 2023 we visited the site at Lainston for some initial tree planting. This is part of the Exclusive Hotels chain, and one of several sites which we have worked on that are owned by that group. Joining us were a small group of corporate volunteers from Open GI Ltd., one of whom had got married at the hotel, and most of the hotel staff also helped, working in shifts, along with the tireless grounds staff who worked all day. The hotel provided an excellent lunch, much to the appreciation of everyone!

We had a little drama as somehow part of our tree delivery mysteriously ended up in a warehouse in Basingstoke, despite being sent first class in plenty of time. Despite frantic phone calls from the nursery and ourselves, and a promise of delivery by the morning of the planting, our remaining saplings failed to appear. We were able to plant the majority  of trees and the excellent grounds people at the hotel planted the final few later in the week. This is the only time saplings have failed to arrive as we always arrange delivery in plenty of time to allow for most unforeseen events.

Overall, 825 cell-grown saplings were planted, a mix of 90 per cent trees and 10 per cent shrubs which will create a natural looking woodland with a canopy of taller trees and shrub understory. Species planted included; Hornbeam, Common Oak, Field Maple, Grey Willow, Hazel, Rowan, Hawthorn, Beech, Wild Service Tree, Crab Apple, and Wild Cherry. All were supported with stakes and guards, and will grow into a fantastic habitat and food source for the wildlife on the site.

The group from Open GI Ltd had a great time. “The organisation, equipment and lunch was all good.  Phil was good fun and made everything interesting and exciting for people, we had fun. Thank you for all your help organising this, we had a good time and learned a lot – hopefully we made a difference too!”
