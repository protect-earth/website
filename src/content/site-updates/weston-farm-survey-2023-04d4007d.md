The first and third of the shelterbelts we planted here are doing very well with very few dead trees. The other shelterbelt was showing a few gaps so we restocked it with a further 433 trees and shrubs to restore it to 100%. Overall, the site is performing well, with a survival rate of 86%, which is impressive after the dry summer the year they were planted. Fortunately, thanks to the wetter summer this year, these trees have received a good boost.
