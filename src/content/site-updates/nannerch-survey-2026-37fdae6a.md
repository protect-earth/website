The survival rate of trees at Nannerch varied considerably across the site, from 90% in one part, right down to 18% in the least successful area. Surviving trees showed very mixed progress with vigorous growth in some areas with trees well out of the guards, and others with very little growth at all. There could be a variety of reasons for this so we will need to monitor this site closely.

Our ecologist visited and provided an in depth report on the wildlife found on the site. Birds included Red Starts, Great Spotted Woodpeckers, Buzzards, Cuckoos and Greenshank. For plants, they found widespread creeping thistle, common harebell, gorse and yarrow.

They also found evidence of larger animals, specifically rabbits; spotting droppings and a burrow, and badgers; finding a badger sett. The badgers are especially important as they are heavily protected under UK law.

Following Oscar’s visit, we returned to Nannerch to carry out a full beat-up survey, marking all the trees that will be restocked in the planting season ahead.
