Site update

In September 2025 a small group of corporate volunteers from Ivanti joined us to carry our some routine maintenance here, led by Phil. They had originally hoped to plant some of the trees last season but regrettably we had to cancel their day due to a severe storm. Fortunately, the weather this time was perfect.

The main task here was weeding because as you can see from the photos the grass grows vigorously here. Volunteers removed grass from inside the guards, mainly by pulling it out. If it had been left the grass would have rotted and damaged the young bark, often resulting in the death of the tree. Where necessary, stakes and guards were straightened too.

This may not sound the most exciting of tasks to volunteer for, but the volunteers had a great time, laughing and joking, and were very enthusiastic once they realised how vital their work was to the survival of all these saplings. We kept them going with hot drinks and midway through the day we stopped for a picnic.

We were very grateful for their hard work.
