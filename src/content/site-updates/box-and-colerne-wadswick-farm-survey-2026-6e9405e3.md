Despite the bramble dominating one area of the site and some flooding during the winter, our surveyor was pleased to see that many of the trees were well out of the tops of their guards and growing strongly. The survival rate of 80% is a little on the low side, but within our guaranteed limit. The Forestry Commission has offered 500 trees to expand on the north side of the road to fill the open spaces created by ash dieback disease so there is no need for us to restock this site at the moment.

There is some maintenance needed here, mainly straightening or replacing broken guards and stakes. The landowner is happy to oversee this with support from local Cotswold Warden volunteers, so we are content to leave it in their capable hands for now. We will continue to monitor the site.
