From time to time we join forces with other environmental charities such as Avon Needs Trees as we all have the same aim and many hands make light work.

Early April is the very last opportunity to plant trees for the season before they start trying to grow, so we were keen to plant these 400 saplings as soon as possible. Grass could be a problem here in the lower paddock, likely to grow and overwhelm the young saplings, so we used biodegradable mulch mats and pegs to suppress it as much as possible.
