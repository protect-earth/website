You may be aware that we installed dormice habitat boxes at High Wood. Dormice normally hibernate in piles of logs or leaves, in the base of trees, and inside tussock grass. When these habitats are in deficit or at risk of disturbance from humans, something we can do to help is create sturdy habitat for them.

We invited a dormouse expert to investigate the boxes. She found that despite a slightly disappointing uptake from the local wildlife, there was evidence that several boxes were occupied, or previously occupied, by the target occupant. One contained shrew droppings. Those not in use were cleaned out in preparation for future occupants.

In one box we found an active mouse very close to the lid, with a possible nest inside. The lid was gently closed in order to prevent the mouse from jumping out. It was not possible to clearly identify the mouse,  without the risk of escapees. Our expert believes it was probably a juvenile dormouse or a wood mouse.
