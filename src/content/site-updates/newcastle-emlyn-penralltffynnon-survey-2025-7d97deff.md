Our site inspector went to survey this site, but it was difficult to find some of our saplings because thick, tall ferns were present across the site. Many of the guards, particularly hedge guards, were covered by ferns, with little light penetrating through. A disappointingly high level of loss was recorded, with 75% dead.

When the fern fronds are small, simply bruising them means they put more vigour into a new stem. The best way of dealing with them is by rolling it, and eventually this rolling of bracken removes it. By the time the trees have surpassed 5 to 10 years in growth, it will shade out the bracken and produce a wonderful understory for wildlife and grasses, etc.
