Towards the end of the same tree planting season we returned to Grove Farm to plant a shelterbelt on the edge of the hotel’s property. Shelterbelts are marvellous for increasing biodiversity, as well as acting as a windbreak and privacy screen.

We had 1000 native trees and shrubs to plant, but fortunately we has a huge turnout of volunteers, both adults and children, who did an amazing job in spite of the mud and the cold. Using canes and spiral guards makes for a quicker job than stakes and work well when planting hedgerows and shelterbelts.

We don’t normally plant fruit trees, but the hotel were keen to have a small orchard, so we planted several species of 100 English apple trees and 100 English pear trees.
