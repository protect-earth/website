Once we had finished sowing the new wildflower meadow we couldn’t wait any longer to visit the two woodland sites we had planted on this farm. Although it was rather early in the year for a site survey we could see that the trees were mostly doing pretty well, with very few dead saplings. The spring buds are beginning to burst open, with the most established trees already outgrowing their guards, especially in the woodland we planted first. 98% survival rate is a remarkable achievement and very encouraging.
