This was our fourth, and thankfully final, attempt to carry out ‘spring’ maintenance at this farm after a storm. excessive temperatures and school holidays had delayed previous attempts. Ten extremely hardy and enthusiastic corporate volunteers from Capsule Insurance Services in Cheltenham turned up to complete the work of straightening and replacing guards, weeding, spreading mulch and checking for dead trees to be replaced next season. They even brought cake!

We had planned an early start to avoid the possible excessive heat, but need not have worried. The weather forecast was a 50/50 chance of rain and of course it rained for most of the morning. Fortunately, our volunteers were well prepared and didn’t let the weather dampen their spirits. We were supposed to finish by 1pm, but we still had a little bit more to do. Our volunteers very kindly stayed for another 45 minutes to finish the work, and then headed off for a very late lunch.

They all seemed to have a great time and are very keen to come back to help us plant more trees here next winter.
