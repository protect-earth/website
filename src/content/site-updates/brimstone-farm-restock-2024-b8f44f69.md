This shelterbelt has fared poorly despite the pre-existing hedgerow sheltering it from the worst of the winds. The tall grass did not help as it will have crowded out some saplings and deprived the others of nutrients.  This autumn, FWAG volunteers returned to plant a further 889 trees and shrubs to bulk out the shelterbelt and fill in the many gaps.
