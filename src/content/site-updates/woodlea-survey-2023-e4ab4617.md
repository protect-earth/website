This was a very busy couple of days for our team, placing mulch mats, dealing with invasive rhododendrons and carrying out a survival survey on our trees here at Woodlea.

The photos show how high the grass has grown and why we needed the mulch mats. Because of the competition from the grass the trees are still quite small but appeared healthy enough. We were pleased to calculate a survival rate of 88% and with the mats in place the saplings should do better.

We were also delighted to discover a numbers of tiny saplings, mainly oak, popping up naturally, and completely undeterred by the long grass.
