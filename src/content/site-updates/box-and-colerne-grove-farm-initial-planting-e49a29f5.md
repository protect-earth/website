We joined forces with the Cotswold Wardens to create a hedgerow on this farm. Our planting day was cold with an early frost, but fortunately the sun came out and warmed the soil sufficiently for us to plant the saplings. Together we planted 240 trees and 60 shrubs.  Planting this little corner between two existing woodlands helps to create a wildlife corridor for the benefit of wildlife.

A fence will be erected to protect the young saplings before allowing livestock back into the field.
