With the help of volunteers, the Protect Earth team led the planting of a 100m hedgerow using  hawthorn, blackthorn, guelder rose, hazel, alder buckthorn; 600 trees and shrubs in total, supported by canes and spirals .As we were planting fairly early in the season we ordered cell grown trees which are a little more expensive but readily available. Nurseries normally lift bare root whips , our usual choice, after the first frosts to ensure that the trees are dormant. This normally occurs around the middle of November, but can be later, so delivery times can be unreliable. We had a very full planting season so this was a good way of making an early start and of being certain that the trees would be available in time.

The weather was glorious for November, and the dogs were friendly, so everyone set to with a will, getting the hedgerow planted in good order before moving on to complete the woodland on the same farm.
