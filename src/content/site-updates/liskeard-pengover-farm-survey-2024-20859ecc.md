While we were in Liskeard for the High Wood Open Day we paid a visit to Pengover Farm just up the way to check the trees we planted in January 2023. The grass was high in most areas, which may explain the rather low survival rate of 77%. Looking down the tubes we found some healthy trees, and we were pleased to see some poking out above the top of the guards too.

We will monitor this site closely with a view to restocking at a later date.
