We visited in August 2023, to check on our trees’ progress and we were very excited to see how well they were doing. Most are now appearing well over the tops of the tubes with berries on some. The grass was tall but didn’t seem to be causing too much of a problem. In fact, the survival rate was surprisingly good, considering the extreme weather over the last couple of years.

The site would benefit from some maintenance so we will plan to do that next summer.
