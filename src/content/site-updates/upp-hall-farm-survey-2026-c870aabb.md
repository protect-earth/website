The landowner very kindly took the photos and carried out this year’s survival survey for us. They reported that the trees have been doing well depite the drought, with no sign of further losses. From the photos we can see rowan, oak and alder doing particularly well, with acorns on some of the oaks. However, some trees have been lost due to  deer debarking.

Despite everything, the survival rate here is still very similar to last year.
