On New Year’s Day some slightly sleepy volunteers turned up to plant a hedgerow on yet another farm in the Box and Colerne area of Wiltshire. Despite the late night for most, together with the Cotswolds Wardens we planted 250 metres of native mixed species hedge and some individual tree planting, a lovely New Year’s gift for the farmer.

The mix was 75% Hawthorn, 25% mix of Hazel, Dogwood, Wayfaring, Purging Buckthorn and Crab Apple at 4 saplings per metre for a stockproof hedge. using spiral rabbit guards  for protection and bamboo canes for support. Other hedges  in the Box/Colerne area are a similar mix.

A small coppice of larger trees was also planted which should expand naturally over time.
