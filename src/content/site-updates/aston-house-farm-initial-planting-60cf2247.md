Protect Earth joined forces with FWAG South West to plant a large shelterbelt on this farm which will act as a windbreak, improve soil conditions, and increase biodiversity as it will provide food and shelter for wildlife. We provided the funding and FWAG provided most of the volunteers, other than our own team. It was a particularly wet and miserable event, but well worth the effort to get these trees planted.

There was a mix of native, broadleaved trees in the centre of the shelterbelt, with shrubs on the outside for a denser, more natural appearance.
