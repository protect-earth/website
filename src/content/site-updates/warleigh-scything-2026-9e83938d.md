The Avon Wildlife Trust very kindly donated some scythes and sponsored two scything workshops for our team. We invited a few others to join us from a Bristol charity too.

The workshops were led by Kai Lange from HORTUS Heart who turned out to be a fantastic instructor. He showed us how to put the scythes together and how to sharpen them correctly before showing us how to use them. We were very lucky with the weather, especially as we had had a whole week of constant rain before the second workshop.

It turns out that neither sharpening scythes nor scything itself is easy (we should have known) but with practice everyone began to show some improvement.

The course participants had a good time and found the workshops useful:

“The scything course today was extremely enjoyable, of course in a beautiful setting.

Kai was passionate whilst also being calm, supportive and kind. I sadly could use a lot more practice to master it but feel that he gave plenty of pointers to help me find my way. He was knowledgeable and clearly experienced. The whole group got engaged and were motivated to keep trying. The equipment provided was good too with plenty of opportunity to sharpen and tinker.”

We had hoped to have some scything session to practise our new skill, but the next few weeks were very wet. We will start earlier next year.
