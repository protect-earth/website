While waiting for our purchase of Warleigh Nature Reserve to complete, we talked to a number of neighbours. This neighbour asked if we could help with some woodland management, especially the removal of tree guards which had been there too long and were too much for her to manage.

We were happy to help out, and together with a group of corporate volunteers from The LandApp, the landowner and her friends we were able to remove the guards. It look a little longer than expected so we didn’t have time to do one or two other little jobs we had planned, so we will visit again in the future to carry our more tasks.

The volunteers gave some positive feedback. “Everyone had a great time and we managed to remove hundreds of tree guards for the land owner. The leaders gave very clear instructions and we were provided with all of the information we needed before the event.”

The landowner was very grateful. “Yesterday was a wonderful day and we had a brilliant time. The volunteers were such a hardworking group and the day was a complete success. They were  a brilliant group of people. It was great to meet Phil’s team and we really appreciated the help.”
