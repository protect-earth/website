We returned to Pennyhill in March 2026 to restock the trees after a very dry summer the previous year. Joining us were volunteers from Chillibob and their clients from Acer. We were even able to accommodate one volunteer who is a wheelchair user thanks to help from the hotel.  Hotel staff  also volunteered again, and together with Phil they planted the new trees. The hotel provided a buffet lunch for the volunteers which was highly appreciated by everyone!

In total we planted 545 new trees, all with stakes and guards. We then revisited the trees we had planted previously, carrying out some basic maintenance to make sure that they continue to do well.
