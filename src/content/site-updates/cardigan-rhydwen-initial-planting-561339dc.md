We planned a woodland and small hedgerow on this site but had to have several attempts to plant here. We had to postpone originally because the ground was frozen solid so impossible to plant trees, the only one we postponed all season. Then it snowed. Our ecologist planted trees on his own on several occasions, then finally completed this project with a handful of local volunteers in mid-February.

Altogether we planted 1,000 trees and 250 shrubs, predominately oak to help maintain the woodland characteristic of Mid/West-Wales, but with a high diversity of other tree species to maximise biodiversity. There are many beech trees thriving in the local area, which we also bolstered by planting more. Surrounding the woodland we put a shrub layer to provide good woodland-edge habitat, and planted with species that provide good sources of nectar, pollen and berries for wildlife.
