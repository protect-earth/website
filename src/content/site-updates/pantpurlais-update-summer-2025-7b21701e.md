We returned to the site outside Llandrindod Wells in Mid Wales in July 2025 to carry out a survival survey of the trees we planted in previous years. Overall, the site is doing amazingly well, with a 95% success rate.

Many of the saplings have put on fantastic growth, especially the oaks. The early pollinator trees like crab apples are four years old now, and top out at 7ft tall. This is great news, as it provides an important food source for pollinator insects early in the year.

Our wildflower meadow hasn’t fared so well, and is very overgrown with grass. We plan to use yellow rattle heavy mix plugs when we maintain to try and break up the grass a bit and give the wildflowers a chance.

The landowner kindly provided some beautiful photographs; the contrast is striking. Even the smallest saplings are starting to look like proper trees now; the guards and stakes are starting to go, roots are down, and the trees are unmistakably at home in the Welsh landscape.
