Steve, our ecologist, visited  Eastcourt Farm on the 11th June to see how the new shelterbelts were progressing. As the trees were planted near to the road this was an easy visit, despite the drizzle. He was pleased to see that the majority of trees were looking healthy, with some trees peeping out of their guards. With a survival rate of 82% there is no need to do anything else here at the moment, but we need to keep a close check on the trees as we are likely to need a small restock in the future.
