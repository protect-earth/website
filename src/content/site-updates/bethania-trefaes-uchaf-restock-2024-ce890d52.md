The landowner had reported a few losses towards the end of 2023 so we planned to replace them while we were in the area planting trees for one of his neighbours.

On a bright February weekend our 25 volunteers finished planting new trees next door and then most of them were able to come and help us with this small restock. The stakes and guards were mostly still in place, and with so many volunteers, the saplings were planted in no time.
