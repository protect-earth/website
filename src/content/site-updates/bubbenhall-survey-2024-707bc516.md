We were keen to return to this beautiful lavender farm to ensure that the recently restocked trees were settling in, and that the mulch mats we laid were doing their job. Our surveyor was delighted to find that nearly all the trees were thriving, with a 95% survival rate. The mulch mats were doing a great job of suppressing grass and most stakes and guards were still upright after last year’s maintenance. We straightened a few others while we were there as this encourages the trees to grow straight and tall.

The wildflower meadow was doing less well. We could see a mix of wildflowers but there were gaps between them. Hopefully, they will self seed to fill in these gaps.  It was good to see a number of butterflies of various species flitting between the plants.
