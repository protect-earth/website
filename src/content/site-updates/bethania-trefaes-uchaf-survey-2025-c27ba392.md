Our surveyor visited this farm to see how the trees are doing, and he was happy to be met with healthy trees and an approximately 85% survival rate, so different from the neighbouring farm which had fared poorly .There were no dead trees found in the hedgerow at all. A 100% survival rate is always hoped for, but rarely found.

The landowner has also planted a few additional trees on the south-west periphery, expanding the impact of our work further. These trees are performing very well, supported by regularly maintained short grass.
