Two corporate teams from AMO Consultancy and TML Partners joined us to help us plant 500 trees to replace trees we had lost. They worked incredibly hard and thanks to a little friendly rivallry they got the work done in short order, leaving us time in the afternoon to tidy up some of the long grass from inside existing guards.

In addition to the saplings we planted, we found  another 100+ self-seeded oaks which we protected with stakes and guards. They were all heavily browsed by deer and looked more like short shrubs than oak trees. Now these struggling oak saplings are guarded and will be able to thrive as part of our woodland.

Of the original 1,500 planted, we recorded a 56.5% survival rate. With 500 replanted and 100 naturally occurring that brings us back to 1,250, which is a cracking result!
