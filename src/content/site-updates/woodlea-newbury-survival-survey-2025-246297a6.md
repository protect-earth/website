The landowner carried out the survey for us this year and took these photographs. We were pleased to see that some of the fast-growing species are already above the protective guard level, some up to 1.5 metres tall. However, the grass is also benefiting from these conditions, overgrowing the mulch mats we laid the year after planting to give our saplings a better chance.

Loss seems concentrated in some patches. Mortality seems to be the highest in the wettest areas where rushes are the densest in the field. Overall, the survival rate is 82%.
