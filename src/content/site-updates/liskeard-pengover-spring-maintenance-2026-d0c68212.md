On a very damp and miserable day in June, Richard and David, our new High Wood Site Managers led six local volunteers and four corporate volunteers from HRA Global in carrying out some basic tree care, removing grass from guards and straightening and replacing stakes and guards as necessary. They also checked for any dead trees which may need replacing in the Autumn.

The kind landowner provided tea, coffee and an excellent lunch. Perhaps the bad weather was an incentive, or the fact that every volunteer who signed up actually turned up (this rarely happens) but we finished a couple of hours earlier than expected.

Our corporate volunteers said that they had a good time and would like to volunteer with us again in the future. “It was a well organised volunteering event, clear instructions and the lady made a delicious lunch! We all really enjoyed it. It was only a few hours and we had factored in a day but think this was due to more volunteers turning up”
