Our site surveyor went to visit this farm hoping that our restocked trees have settled in well as the weather this year had been wetter and cooler. He found that on the whole, both the original trees and the more recently restocked trees were doing fairly well with an overall survival rate of 78%, just below our guaranteed 80%.

The problems on this site is the heavy clay soil which tends to crack when very dry, and also the vigorously growing grass in some areas which competes with the trees for space and nutrients.

Most of the surviving trees are doing well with healthy green leaves and steady growth.
