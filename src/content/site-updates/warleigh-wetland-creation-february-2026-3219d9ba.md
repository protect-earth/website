We were very keen to start work developing the wetlands so as soon we had finished clearing barbed wire and creating pathways through the nature reserve we turned our attention to the area nearer the River Avon, a rare chalk stream.

The team began by blocking the land drains and building leaky dams in old drainage trenches to slow the water down, encouraging ponds to form.

A few days later we sent up a drone to get some bird’s eye views, and we could already see a big difference.

We were excited to find toads and other wildlife moving in soon after that, and water was now staying on the land for weeks after rain events instead of just days.
