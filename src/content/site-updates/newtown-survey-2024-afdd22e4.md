Our ecologist went to visit this relatively new site in May 2024 for the first time since the trees were planted. He was very pleased to see how well the saplings had settled into their new home. Carrying out this survey he was delighted to discover a survival rate of 92%. with survivors looking strong and healthy, and with a few growing out of the top of the guards.

The landowner was able to carry out some maintenance so nothing else needs to be done on this site for the time being.
