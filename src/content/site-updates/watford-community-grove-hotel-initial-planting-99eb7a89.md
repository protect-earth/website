This planting at a hotel near Watford is one of our larger projects, with 3600 trees planned over the next two years. In November, we decided to begin by planting 400 trees on the site.  We chose a good mix of species, including small-leaf lime, wild cherry, silver birch, hawthorn, hazel and dogwood to start us off.

The hotel have their own gardeners, so these trees should be well cared for.
