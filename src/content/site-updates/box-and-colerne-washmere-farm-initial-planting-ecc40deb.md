This was the second of two Wiltshire farms planted on New Year’s Day in conjunction with the Cotswold Wardens, a very positive start for 2022. We planted 320 mixed species trees and shrubs, predominantly hawthorn,  to create just shy of 100m of hedgerow. The new saplings were protected from rabbits and other gnawing mammals by clear spiral guards and were supported by bamboo canes.
