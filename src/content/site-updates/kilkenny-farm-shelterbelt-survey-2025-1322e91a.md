The landowner very kindly sent us a video and these photos of his shelterbelt. We were pleased to see that most surviving trees were well out of their guards although they could be seen being blown in the wind. He said that they had some problems with deer who nibbled the trees and knocked over trees and guards. Some trees were clearly dead and there was one larger gap in the shelterbelt where trees were struggling. However, he was quite happy with the shelterbelt overall and estimated a survival rate of around 80%.

We will make sure we carry out a survey ourselves next year as the survival rate is borderline at 80%.  We will probably need to restock the gap too.
