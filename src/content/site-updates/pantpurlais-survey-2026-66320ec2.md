Overall this site is in excellent condition and has clearly been very well cared for by the landowner. The trees planted first are now very large and healthy, and are well established. The trees planted later are smaller, which is to be expected, but they are still doing extremely well. All other planting areas (for example, a small patch of oak planted on a slope by the house, and a couple of rows of saplings planted along a pathway) have almost no dead trees at all.

Deer are present on-site and have attacked saplings, but the problem is not severe and has not lead to concerning losses in the areas planted by us. Our team only saw one sapling killed by deer in our planting areas.

The landowner has found using wood chip as a weed suppressant at the base of saplings very successful, especially if it's been left outside for a year to grow fungal colonies prior to use.

Disappointingly, the meadow has not done well. It has failed to support wildflowers even after being re-sown a second time with a rattle-heavy seed mix. The problem here is the vigorous grass growth, so we need to give this a lot more thought before making a third attempt to establish this wildflower meadow.

The landowner has reported seeing kites, buzzards, roe deer and muntjac deer on or above his property.
