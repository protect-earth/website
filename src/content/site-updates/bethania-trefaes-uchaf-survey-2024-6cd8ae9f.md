On a cool, overcast day in June our ecologist went to visit this site to check both the original trees, and the recently restocked saplings too. Thanks to the landowner’s regular maintenance, especially keeping the grass down, this site was looking promising, with a survival rate of 86%. The earlier planted trees were beginning to clear the top of the guards, and most of the new ones were producing plenty of leaves too.

No follow up required here this year.
